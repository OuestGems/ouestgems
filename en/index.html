Selected directly
at the source

Through several journeys to Pakistan, our team of professionals discovered exceptional craftsmanship and emeralds of remarkable quality. We created Ouest Gems Export to offer stones selected directly at the source, with constant attention to quality, traceability and transparency.

Workshop presence

Our field visits allow us to observe stones, exchange with local specialists and anchor each selection in a documented process.
